Upload a short video of one product. A model running on this computer looks at four frames and suggests a product type, colour and appearance. No API key, paid service, audio transcription or social-media scraping.

Offline tools or model files are not installed yet. On the application computer, run powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/setup-video.ps1, then restart the app. Manual product entry remains available.

Product:

Up to 30 MB, 60 seconds and 1920×1080 pixels (or 1080×1920). Keep the product clearly visible. One product per clip; avoid rapid cuts, heavy overlays and multiple outfits.

YOUR VIDEO STAYS PRIVATE

Look. Suggest. You decide.

  1. Four representative frames are extracted locally.
  2. A pretrained visual model compares them with a fixed fashion vocabulary.
  3. You approve only the useful tags.
  4. Review the product form and save it to make the keywords searchable.

The original upload and temporary frames are deleted after processing. They are never published or sent to a hosted AI service.

Suggestions can be wrong. The model does not establish fabric composition, stock, price, identity or purchase verification. CPU processing may take longer on the first run.

One analysis at a time per seller; ten uploads per seller in 24 hours. The local worker processes one video at a time with a short bounded queue.
